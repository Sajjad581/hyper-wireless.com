Workflow

From commit to certification in six steps

01

Upload build

Push firmware to your build repository over CLI, API, or Web.

02

Reserve tester

Schedule an available AEON tester (SDR + RF chamber) in a region.

03

Secure tunnel

Your workstation connects via mutual-TLS to the reserved lab.

04

Deploy & run

The tester flashes your UE and executes the selected TTCN-3 campaign.

05

Live monitor

Stream verdicts, MSCs, and logs. Pause, resume, or cancel any run.

06

Report & analyze

Download certification reports. Ask the Copilot why a testcase failed.
